Privacy policy

This policy covers the Print Shop seller tool, a private application used by one eBay seller in Australia to manage their own made-to-order 3D-printed products. It is not offered to other sellers or the public.

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

What the tool accesses

With the seller's permission, given through eBay's sign-in page, the tool reads and manages the seller's own listings, orders, offers and listing performance figures. For each order it receives the details eBay provides to sellers to fill the order: the buyer's name, delivery address, the items bought and any personalisation text the buyer supplied.

How the information is used

Buyer details are used only to make, pack and post the order the buyer placed, and to answer questions about that order. Personalisation text is used only to make the buyer's item.

Where it is kept

Information is stored on the seller's own private server in Australia. It is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising or marketing.

How long it is kept

Order and buyer details are kept for as long as needed to complete the order, handle returns and meet Australian tax record-keeping rules (up to five years), then deleted.

Your choices

Buyers can ask about or request deletion of their information by sending the seller a message through eBay. Requests are answered within 30 days, subject to any legal record-keeping obligations.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
